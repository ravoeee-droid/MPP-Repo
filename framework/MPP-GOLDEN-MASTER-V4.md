## Human-First Creative Strategy
Every important section must define:
- real target person
- current feeling / trigger
- fear / objection
- desired transformation
- proof needed at the point of doubt
- primary action
- secondary action if truly needed

The website is a decision experience, not a collection of sections.

## Required artifacts
- Facts Ledger
- Positioning Thesis
- Art Direction Lock
- 3+ Signature Components
- primary + secondary conversion journey
- Trust OS
- Motion System
- Asset Manifest
- desktop/mobile QA evidence
- Release Evidence

## MPP thesis
MPP must not look or sound like a generic recruiting agency.

Core system:
- PEOPLE — right people in the right roles
- PERFORMANCE — leadership / sales / execution that produces results
- SYSTEMS — roles, processes, structures and responsibility that make growth durable

North Star:
> Die richtigen Menschen sind erst der Anfang.

Brand system:
> People. Performance. Systems.

Operating Line:
> PEOPLE → PERFORMANCE → SYSTEMS → GROWTH

## Signature Components
1. MPP Operating Line — scroll-linked People → Performance → Systems → Growth
2. MPP Growth Diagnostic — 5-question interactive diagnostic
3. MPP Digital Consultant “Mia” — clearly labelled AI assistant
4. Founder Experience — real human trust anchor

## Art Direction Lock
- warm Ivory
- deep Ink
- Signal Citron
- Graphite / Mist
- Swiss/editorial layout discipline
- large editorial statements
- clean grotesk UI/body type
- documentary human photography
- thin rules / borders
- generous white space
- controlled asymmetry

No-go:
- recruiter blue as generic default
- black/gold fake luxury
- random bento walls
- gradient orbs
- glow everywhere
- skyscraper = premium cliché
- stock handshakes
- random 3D
- endless marquee
- repeated identical card grids

## STOP-SHIP
Hard fail if:
- visual direction drifts
- random redesign
- fake proof
- fake founder/customer representation
- broken image
- wrong logo source
- mobile overflow
- generic / weak hero
- missing primary conversion
- unverified certification shown as real
- giant unexplained whitespace
- clipping / overlap
- obvious AI-template smell
- motion disappears during a local fix
- desktop fix damages mobile
- final deploy happens without human approval
