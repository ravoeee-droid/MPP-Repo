## Research workflow per client

Create four separate reference buckets. Never use a single website as „the reference“.

### Bucket 1 — Composition
Collect 3 examples for grid, whitespace, section rhythm and content density.

### Bucket 2 — Typography
Collect 3 examples for headline/body relationship, type scale and expressive details.

### Bucket 3 — Interaction
Collect 2–3 examples for motion, scroll behavior or a relevant signature interaction.

### Bucket 4 — Brand world
Collect references from outside web design too: architecture, packaging, editorial, photography, materials, vehicles, fashion, industrial design, etc.

## Synthesis

After collecting references, write down patterns without screenshots:
- dominant layout principle
- density
- typography contrast
- image treatment
- shape language
- motion behavior
- color behavior

Then design from the written synthesis. This reduces copying and produces a more original result.

## Competitive scan

For 5–10 direct competitors capture:
- hero promise
- CTA
- proof type
- offer structure
- visual clichés
- content gaps
- mobile weaknesses

The goal is not to mimic the strongest competitor. The goal is to identify where the category is visually and verbally interchangeable, then choose a defensible difference.
