# CONTENT INBOX

Drop client-supplied or approved content here before it is normalized into final copy.

## Needed before final release

### Company facts
- official company name / legal entity
- address / locations
- contact details
- team/founder details
- official service descriptions

### Proof
- Google profile link
- current review rating + count
- approved review quotes
- approved customer logos
- case studies
- measurable outcomes
- testimonials
- certifications / memberships / awards
- BAFA / funding evidence if applicable

### Founder
- exact full name
- role/title
- approved biography
- relevant career milestones
- approved portrait(s)

### Conversion
- booking/calendar destination
- contact recipient
- CRM / email routing
- form fields
- privacy / consent requirements

### AI assistant
- approved knowledge base
- forbidden topics / escalation rules
- human handoff contact
- booking rules

## Rule
Do not silently convert unverified notes into public claims. First move facts into `FACTS-LEDGER.md` with evidence/status.
