# MPP PROJECT BRIEF

## Project
Rebuild / reposition the public website for MPP.

Source:
https://www.mpp-recruit.com/

## Strategic objective
Move perception away from a conventional recruitment provider toward a premium entrepreneurial advisory/growth system centered on People, Performance and Systems.

## Brand idea
**People. Performance. Systems.**

## Hero
**Die richtigen Menschen sind erst der Anfang.**

Supporting idea:
MPP brings the right people into the right roles, strengthens performance and builds structures in which sustainable growth becomes possible.

Final public copy must be aligned with verified MPP services and client approval.

## Desired perception
- human
- progressive
- precise
- entrepreneurial
- credible
- high-end without fake luxury
- operational, not theoretical
- boutique, not anonymous corporate consulting

## Category enemy
Avoid:
- transactional recruiting-only perception
- generic HR language
- consulting buzzwords without proof
- recruiter stock photography
- empty “quality / innovation / personal” claims without mechanism

## Audiences
Primary:
- owners / managing directors
- decision-makers in growing SMEs
- leaders with recruiting, performance/sales or organisational bottlenecks

Secondary:
- candidates / talent via a separate candidate journey when strategically relevant

## Conversion
Primary: qualified first conversation  
Secondary: MPP Growth Diagnostic

## Trust drivers
- real founder authority
- verified reviews
- approved client references
- case studies with evidence
- transparent process
- exact credentials/funding only when verified
- confidentiality
- thought leadership

## Fact rule
Anything about customers, results, certifications, funding, team, founder CV, jobs or ratings stays UNVERIFIED until evidence is added to the Facts Ledger.
